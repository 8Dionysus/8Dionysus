# AGENTS.md

Root route card for the `8Dionysus` public entry repository and its shared-root
projection. Keep this inherited surface short; use nested cards for local deltas
and open depth documents only when the task needs them.

## Ownership

`8Dionysus` owns public/profile orientation, glossary alignment, workspace entry
and AGENTS-map audit surfaces, plus the checked-in sources for selected
shared-root files (`AGENTS.md`, `docs/WORKSPACE_ROOT_ENTRY.md`,
`AOA_WORKSPACE_ROOT`, `.agents/`, and the admitted `.codex/` subset).

It does not own ecosystem doctrine, authored ToS meaning, runtime behavior, SDK
contracts, shared skills, techniques, evals, memory, KAG, playbooks, agents,
stats, or model-formation canon. Those remain in their owner repositories.
Generated, installed, compact, runtime, and adapter views are evidence or
transport, never substitute authority.

## Work contract

- Start from this card and the nearest `AGENTS.md` for each touched path. A
  nested card adds local ownership, risk, and routes; it does not repeat the
  root.
- Choose the owner before editing. Change authored source or declared builder
  inputs, regenerate derived views, and report source change, regeneration,
  verification, and unverified boundaries separately.
- Do not preload nearby documentation. Open `README.md` only when human use,
  public presentation, or documented topology is relevant.
- Keep agency, recurrence, quest, progression, checkpoint, and growth claims
  bounded, evidence-linked, reviewable, and reversible.
- Record a durable owner-local decision when future agents need the rationale
  for a structural, ownership, route-law, validator-authority, public-contract,
  or topology choice. Otherwise state that no decision record was needed.

## Route by intent

- Public/profile orientation: `README.md`, then `docs/START_HERE.md`; posture or
  glossary changes route to `docs/PUBLIC_ENTRY_POSTURE.md` or `GLOSSARY.md`.
- Shared-root projection: `docs/WORKSPACE_INSTALL.md`; Codex regeneration:
  `docs/CODEX_PLANE_REGENERATION.md`; detailed inherited history:
  `docs/AGENTS_ROOT_REFERENCE.md`.
- Ecosystem charter and federation: `Agents-of-Abyss`; authored knowledge and
  interpretation: `Tree-of-Sophia`; runtime and deployment: `abyss-stack`;
  typed discovery/routing/dispatch: `aoa-sdk`.
- Companion behavior and safe operator automation: `ATM10-Agent`; voice-first
  interview and reviewed portrait projections: `Dionysus`; reusable practice:
  `aoa-techniques`; portable bundles: `aoa-skills`; proof: `aoa-evals`.
- Durable memory: `aoa-memo`; session evidence: `aoa-session-memory`; derived
  knowledge: `aoa-kag`; recurring scenario composition: `aoa-playbooks`; role,
  handoff, and progression contracts: `aoa-agents`; model-formation lineage:
  `aoa-agon`; measurement grammar and federation: `aoa-stats`.

  provenance, freshness, missingness, and non-executing action intents without
  taking owner authority.

The deprecated `aoa-routing` predecessor is a maintenance-only rollback
surface. Do not route new navigation or dispatch work there; archiving it
requires a separate operator decision.

## Memory and skill boundaries

Use `aoa_memo` for reviewed continuity and recall; use `aoa_session_memory` only
for read-only evidence about a stable operational entity. Raw session authority
remains in `.aoa`; writeback, repair, reindexing, distillation, and promotion
stay with their named owners. Workspace memory-map status is a generated route:
rebuild it through `scripts/build_workspace_memory_map.py`, do not infer it.

`aoa skills` is passive and exact. The inspection commands live on demand in
`VALIDATION.md#skill-inspection`. Semantic skill retrieval belongs to `aoa-kag`;
SDK inspection and `aoa surfaces detect` remain read-only and never select,
activate, or execute a skill. Diagnose a concrete skill/MCP/tool delivery
mismatch through the repo-local `aoa-workspace-diagnose` home skill. Diagnosis
does not authorize repair, install, restart, configuration mutation, or another
owner's change. Risky mutation requires the nearest owner contract, applicable
runtime boundary, and explicit host or human confirmation.

## Projection stop-lines

Follow `docs/WORKSPACE_INSTALL.md` and
`docs/CODEX_PLANE_REGENERATION.md`. In particular:

- edit the source-owned copy under `<workspace-root>/8Dionysus/` first; do not treat the live copies as source
- keep the repository `README.md` profile-owned and GitHub-facing, and project `docs/WORKSPACE_ROOT_ENTRY.md` separately as `<workspace-root>/README.md`
- never copy or prune `<workspace-root>/.agents/skills/`; shared bundles install
  through `aoa-skills`
- keep live `.codex/config.toml` and `.codex/agents/` outside the generic
  projector and use their dedicated owner routes
- use a clean current owner checkout; non-current execution is branch-trial
  evidence, not owner-current proof
- route generated or install drift to the owner repo

For AGENTS-map and workspace audits, use the applicable on-demand route in
`VALIDATION.md#agents-map-and-workspace-audit`, distinguishing a public-baseline
pass from a sibling-workspace pass.

## Landing and verification

`.github/AGENTS.md` owns GitHub-native details. Begin from current
`origin/main`, preserve unrelated dirty work, and keep the intended diff exact.
A PR names changed surfaces, checks, skips, and risk. Merge only after required
checks, including `Repo Validation`, are observed green; if status or permission
cannot be observed, stop.

Use the narrow route in `VALIDATION.md` for the changed surface. For `stats/`,
also use its local validation route. When projection, hook, plugin, convergence,
or closeout behavior changes, consult `docs/AGENTS_ROOT_REFERENCE.md` and its
named helper. Keep `aoa-codex-doctor`, `aoa-codex-status`, and
`aoa-codex-bootstrap` discoverable wrappers, and
`aoa_codex_convergence_report.{json,md}` as evidence only. The rule remains:
convergence reports are evidence, not authority.
